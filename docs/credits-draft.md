## English

Built by [Hope Chen], with Claude (Anthropic) as a coding and research assistant. Inspired by The New York Times' election needle.

**Data.** Results and polling-station report times: Central Election Commission (中選會) election database and published attachments; file hashes are checked against the CEC's. County boundaries: taiwan-atlas (MIT licence), derived from the Ministry of the Interior's 直轄市、縣市界線 (Open Government Data License, data.gov.tw/dataset/7442). TV vote counts in the 2024 count watch were read off the channels' public YouTube recordings (公視, 華視, 三立, TVBS); only the numbers are used. Polls and race backgrounds on the entry page are credited where they appear.

**Type.** Noto Serif TC and Noto Sans TC (Google, SIL Open Font License) and IBM Plex Sans Condensed (IBM, SIL Open Font License), via Google Fonts.

**Taiwanese (台文).** Draft translation following the Ministry of Education's 臺灣台語常用詞辭典 writing, reviewed by [names]. Until then it is marked as a draft.

**Code and data** are open: github.com/hopehhchen/twn-watch-party-2026. Corrections are welcome there.

A replay of past election nights, built from the CEC's own records and from what TV channels showed on screen. Counts from sources that only report real ballots set a floor the final can't go below. Commercial TV counts are shown as a field average with their spread.

How to read this

Floor. The highest count among reliable sources (the CEC, 公視's own count, 華視). Real ballots only ever add up, so the final is at least this number.

Field. Commercial channels (2024: 三立, TVBS; 2022: 東森, 民視, 台視). Their early numbers are partly projections, so they are shown as an average with a range, never as a floor.

Final. Shown only because this is a replay. On the night itself the final is unknown; the page would instead flag any source that later turns out to have shown more than the final, after a person has checked the broadcast frame.

Projection (in development, not shown yet). A win probability for each race's two main contenders, estimated from how the stations already counted have swung since earlier elections. Tested on the 2018 and 2022 nights with polling-station results. It will only be as sharp as the CEC's live data: if the 2026 feed gives only district (行政區) totals, as opposed to per-station results, the projection still works but is noticeably less certain, especially early in the count, and the page will say so. Electors per polling station are not published before the vote (the CEC announces citywide totals three days before), so the projection estimates them from past elections until each station reports.

Data: CEC election database (db.cec.gov.tw); channel streams read frame by frame from YouTube recordings (2024); Wayback Machine captures of 自由時報's comparison table (2022). Times are Taipei time.

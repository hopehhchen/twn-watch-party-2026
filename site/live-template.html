About · 說明 Results · 開票

A replay of real election nights from the CEC's own records, with the projection model run as if live. Pick a night, press play, open any race. Nothing here is simulated, and nothing from later in the night leaks in: every chart and axis only uses what had been counted by the time on the clock.

以中選會的原始紀錄重播真實的開票夜，預測模型如同現場即時運算。選一個晚上、按下播放、點開任何一場選舉。這裡沒有模擬數據，也不會用到之後才開出的結果：每張圖、每條座標軸都只用到時鐘當下已開出的票。

How to read this · 怎麼看

The dial is the chance each of a race's two main contenders wins, from the votes counted so far. Everyone else's votes are set aside. It starts wide and narrows as stations report. Decided means the lead is larger than every remaining elector could overturn, with no assumption about turnout.

How it is worked out. The stations already counted are compared with how the same villages voted before (for mayors, the previous mayoral and presidential races, averaged; for president, the previous presidential race). That swing is applied to the stations still out, with room for districts to move differently. See the About page for how well it did.

Top panel. Mayoral nights: one block per race, colored by the projected leader and shaded by how sure the model is (as on the map), with counts by party and how many each won last time; hover a block for the race, click it to open it below. Presidential nights: the national race itself, with its chance and margin charts (and, for 2024, the count watch).

Three-way races (2024: eight counties where 柯文哲 was close): the dial shows the two candidates likeliest to win, and the third candidate's chance is given beside it. The chances come from the projected shares and their uncertainty, and start at the 5th station counted. Count watch (2024): per candidate, the floor (highest count among the CEC and the reliable channels), the other channels' range, and the model's projected final with its 80% range.

Map. Before a race has any counted stations, its county is hatched in the party color of the incumbent. Once counting starts, it takes the color of the projected leader's own party (gray for independents, teal for 台灣民眾黨), shaded from light (toss-up) to solid (decided). 金門, 連江 and 澎湖 are drawn in insets.

Turnout view. "Show: Turnout" switches the cards, map and race panel to the projected final turnout, worked out the same way as the vote swing. On the 2018 and 2022 nights it was within about 2 points by the time 5–10% of stations were in. The "Ballots counted" line uses it to estimate what share of all ballots has been counted, a better progress measure than stations, since stations differ in size.

Council view (2018, 2022). Who would hold each council seat if counting stopped now. Each district elects several members; the candidates with the most votes win. A district electing 4 reserves a seat for a woman, plus one per 4 more seats (地方制度法 §33); "women's seat" marks a woman elected this way, "displaced" the man who loses his place. A seat is decided once even the most damaging split of the votes still out (every remaining elector voting, all for the closest challengers) couldn't change who holds it. Nothing is projected here.

Jitter. With "Jitter" on, the needle swings across the middle of its range of outcomes each time the estimate moves, then settles on the estimate within a few seconds.

Feed. "Per station" is what the model can do with polling-station results. "District totals" shows what happens if the CEC's live feed only gives district (行政區) totals: the same model, noticeably less certain early on. Which one the 2026 feed provides is not yet known.

Caveats. The two contenders are taken from the final result here; for 2026 they are set in advance. Times are when the CEC logged each station's report (all contests at a station at once), which can trail what TV showed. 2018 counting ran past midnight in many places because referendums were counted the same night.

指針是每場選舉兩位主要候選人各自勝選的機率，依目前已開出的票計算，其他候選人的票先不計。一開始範圍很寬，隨著投開票所回報逐漸收斂。確定表示領先票數已超過所有尚未開票的選舉人數，不需要任何投票率假設。

怎麼算的。把已開出的投開票所，和同一村里過去的投票結果比較（縣市長：上一屆縣市長與總統選舉的平均；總統：上一屆總統選舉），把這個「擺盪」套用到尚未開出的投開票所，並允許各區有不同的變化。模型過去的準確度請見說明頁。

上方面板。縣市長選舉夜：每場選舉一格，顏色為預估領先者的政黨，深淺代表模型的把握（與地圖相同），並列出各黨領先數與上屆當選數；滑鼠移到格子上可看該場選舉，點一下即在下方打開。總統選舉夜：全國選情本身，附勝率與差距走勢圖（2024 年另有開票數字監看）。

三強鼎立（2024 年柯文哲票數接近的八個縣市）：指針顯示最可能勝選的兩人，第三人的勝率另外標示。勝率來自預估得票率及其不確定性，從第 5 個投開票所開出後才顯示。開票數字監看（2024）：每位候選人的「下限」（中選會與可靠頻道中的最高票數）、其他頻道的範圍，以及模型預估的最終票數與 80% 區間。

地圖。尚未開出任何投開票所時，以斜線標示現任者的政黨顏色。開票後改為預估領先者本人的政黨顏色（無黨籍為灰色、台灣民眾黨為青綠色），由淺（難分高下）到深（確定）。金門、連江與澎湖畫在小框內。

投票率檢視。「顯示：投票率」會把卡片、地圖與選舉面板換成預估的最終投票率，算法與得票擺盪相同。在 2018 與 2022 年，開出 5–10% 投開票所時誤差約 2 個百分點以內。「已開票數」一行用它來估計全部選票已開出的比例；由於各投開票所大小不同，這比投開票所數更能反映進度。

議員檢視（2018、2022）。如果此刻停止開票，每個議員席次會由誰拿下。每個選區應選數名，得票最高者當選。應選 4 名的選區保障 1 席婦女名額，每增 4 名再增 1 席（地方制度法第 33 條）；「婦女保障」標示依此當選的女性，「被排擠」標示因此落選的男性。即使剩餘票數以最不利的方式分配（所有未開票的選舉人都投票，且全投給最接近的挑戰者）也無法改變當選人時，該席為確定。這裡不做任何預測。

抖動。開啟「抖動」後，每當估計有變化，指針會在可能結果的中段範圍內擺動，幾秒內再回到估計值。

資料。「逐所」是有投開票所層級結果時模型的表現；「鄉鎮市區」顯示若中選會即時資料只有鄉鎮市區合計時的情形：同一個模型，早期明顯較不確定。2026 年的即時資料會提供哪一種，目前還不知道。

限制。這裡的兩位主要候選人取自最終結果；2026 年會事先設定。時間是中選會登錄各投開票所回報的時間（同一所的所有選舉一起登錄），可能比電視畫面晚。2018 年因同晚開公投票，許多地方開票到午夜以後。

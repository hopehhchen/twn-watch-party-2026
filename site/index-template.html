Who's going to win, while the votes are still being counted

票還在開，誰會贏？

On 28 November 2026 Taiwan elects its 22 mayors and county magistrates, and every city and county council. This site follows the count as it comes in: for each race, a needle with the chance each contender wins, the projected margin and turnout, and who would hold each council seat if counting stopped now. Until then it replays real past nights — 2018 and 2022 local, 2020 and 2024 presidential — minute by minute, exactly as the model would have seen them.

2026 年 11 月 28 日，台灣將選出 22 位縣市長，以及各縣市議員。本站在開票過程中即時追蹤：每場縣市長選舉有一支指針，顯示各候選人的勝選機率、預估差距與投票率；議員則顯示若此刻停止開票、各席會由誰拿下。在那之前，本站以真實的過去開票夜（2018、2022 地方選舉，2020、2024 總統選舉）逐分鐘重播，完全照模型當時能看到的資料運算。

How it works · 怎麼算

The Central Election Commission (CEC) reports results polling station by polling station. Each station covers a few villages (村里), and every village has voted before. The model leans on that.

  1. Two sides. Each mayoral race is scored as one number: the KMT contender's share of the top two (or, where the KMT fields no one, the share of whoever faces the DPP). Other candidates are set aside; races where a third candidate is close get a three-way treatment (below).
  2. A baseline for every village. How the same village split last time: for mayors, the previous mayoral and presidential races averaged; for president, the previous presidential race. Stations are matched by village, so renumbered, split or merged stations don't matter (98–100% of electors match).
  3. The swing so far. At the stations already counted, how far tonight's share has moved from the baseline, city-wide and district by district. That swing is applied to every station still out.
  4. What's left. Each station still out has a known number of electors; the model estimates how many will have voted from the turnout at counted stations compared with their own past turnout.
  5. Uncertainty. The projected margin has a range that allows for the whole city swinging differently, for districts differing, and for station-to-station noise. The win chance comes from that range. It starts wide and narrows as stations report.
  6. Decided. When a lead is bigger than every elector at the stations still out, the race is decided, whatever the turnout.

Three-way races

When a third candidate is close (in 2024, 柯文哲 in eight counties), the two candidates with a past election to compare with are split by the swing model, and the third candidate's share is projected from the counted stations in each district, adjusted for station size. The page shows a two-way needle between the two likeliest winners and the third candidate's chance beside it, from the 5th station counted.

Turnout, council seats and the count watch

Turnout is projected the same way as the vote: counted stations against their own past turnout. Council seats aren't projected: the page shows who would hold each seat if counting stopped now, with the women's quota, and marks a seat decided only when no split of the votes still out could change it. The count watch (2024) sets the TV channels' on-screen counts next to the CEC count and the model's projected final, and flags any count that goes above what can plausibly be reached.

中央選舉委員會（中選會）以投開票所為單位公布結果。每個投開票所涵蓋幾個村里，而每個村里過去都投過票。模型就是利用這一點。

  1. 兩方。每場縣市長選舉化為一個數字：國民黨候選人在前兩名中的得票比例（國民黨未提名時，則為與民進黨對決者的比例）。其他候選人先不計；第三人票數接近的選舉另以三強方式處理（見下）。
  2. 每個村里的基準。同一村里上次怎麼投：縣市長選舉取上一屆縣市長與總統選舉的平均；總統選舉取上一屆總統選舉。投開票所以村里對應，所以投開票所重新編號、拆分或合併都不影響（98–100% 的選舉人可直接對應）。
  3. 目前的擺盪。在已開出的投開票所，比較今晚的比例與基準差了多少，分全市與各區計算，再把這個擺盪套用到每個尚未開出的投開票所。
  4. 剩下多少。每個尚未開出的投開票所，選舉人數是已知的；投票人數則依已開投開票所與其過去投票率的比較來估計。
  5. 不確定性。預估差距有一個區間，涵蓋全市整體擺盪不同、各區不同，以及投開票所之間的雜訊。勝率就從這個區間算出：一開始很寬，隨投開票所回報而收斂。
  6. 確定。當領先票數大於所有尚未開票投開票所的選舉人數，不論投票率如何，勝負已定。

三強鼎立

第三位候選人票數接近時（2024 年柯文哲在八個縣市），有過去選舉可比較的兩人依擺盪模型拆分，第三人的得票率則依各區已開投開票所推估，並依投開票所規模調整。頁面顯示最可能勝選兩人之間的指針，第三人的勝率另外標示，從第 5 個投開票所開出後開始。

投票率、議員席次與開票數字監看

投票率的推估方式與得票相同：已開投開票所與其自身過去投票率比較。議員席次不做預測：頁面顯示若此刻停止開票、各席會由誰拿下（計入婦女保障名額），只有在剩餘票數無論如何分配都無法改變結果時才標示為確定。開票數字監看（2024）把各電視台畫面上的票數與中選會票數、模型預估的最終票數並列，若有票數超出合理可能達到的範圍就會標示出來。

What it can't do · 限制

  • Small sample. Four nights, 87 county races, few of them close. The calibration numbers above are rough.
  • The 2026 feed. Everything above uses per-station results. If the CEC's live feed on the night only gives district (鄉鎮市區) totals, the same model runs but is noticeably less certain early on (the "District totals" switch on the results page shows how much). Which one the feed provides isn't known yet.
  • Station electors. Not published before the vote, so they are estimated from 2022 and 2024 station data until a station reports.
  • Report times are when the CEC logged each station, for all contests at once. TV often showed counts earlier.
  • Three-way races can't be checked well: one night, eight counties.
  • 樣本小。四個晚上、87 場縣市層級選舉，接戰的不多。上面的校準數字只是粗略參考。
  • 2026 年的即時資料。以上都使用逐所結果。若當晚中選會的即時資料只有鄉鎮市區合計，同一模型仍可運作，但早期明顯較不確定（開票頁的「鄉鎮市區」開關可看出差多少）。目前還不知道會提供哪一種。
  • 各投開票所選舉人數投票前不公布，因此在投開票所回報前，以 2022、2024 年的資料估計。
  • 回報時間是中選會登錄各投開票所的時間（同一所的所有選舉一起）。電視常常更早顯示票數。
  • 三強選舉難以驗證：只有一晚、八個縣市。

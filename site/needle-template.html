Sources replay Needle

A replay of real election nights from the CEC's own records, with the projection model run as if live. Pick a night, press play, open any race. Nothing here is simulated, and nothing from later in the night leaks in: every chart and axis only uses what had been counted by the time on the clock.

How to read this

The dial is the chance each of a race's two main contenders wins, from the votes counted so far. Everyone else's votes are set aside. It starts wide and narrows as stations report. Decided means the lead is larger than every remaining elector could overturn, with no assumption about turnout.

How it is worked out. The stations already counted are compared with how the same villages voted before (the previous mayoral and presidential races, averaged). That swing is applied to the stations still out, with room for districts to move differently. Tested on the 2018 and 2022 nights; see docs/needle.md in the repository.

Map. Before a race has any counted stations, its county is hatched in the party color of the incumbent mayor. Once counting starts, it takes the color of the projected leader's own party (gray for independents, teal for 台灣民眾黨), shaded from light (toss-up) to solid (decided). 金門, 連江 and 澎湖 are drawn in insets.

Turnout view. "Show: Turnout" switches the cards, map and race panel to the projected final turnout, worked out the same way as the vote swing: counted stations against their own turnout last time, applied to the stations still out. On the 2018 and 2022 nights it was within about 2 points by the time 5–10% of stations were in, against about 3.7 points for simply assuming last time's turnout. The "Ballots counted" line uses it to estimate what share of all ballots has been counted, which is a better progress measure than stations, since stations differ in size.

Feed. "Per station" is what the model can do with polling-station results. "District totals" shows what happens if the CEC's live feed only gives district (行政區) totals: the same model, noticeably less certain early on. Which one the 2026 feed provides is not yet known.

Turnout. The live estimate compares turnout at the stations already in with their own past turnout. The slider replaces it with a scenario (in standard deviations of past turnout changes) for the votes-left figures only; it barely moves the win probability, because turnout scales both sides.

Caveats. The two contenders are taken from the final result here; for 2026 they are set in advance. Times are when the CEC logged each station's report, which can trail what TV showed. 2018 counting ran past midnight in many places because referendums were counted the same night.
